Modified for DERIVA: topology-preserving simplification (0.001 degrees in the
western Mediterranean and audited regional patches of New York, Singapore and
Eilat, 0.04 degrees elsewhere), exterior rings only, five decimal source vertex
rounding, omission of minute polygons outside the detailed regions. Calculated
intersections at the rectangular patch limits retain their computed precision.
Detailed region bounds [west, south, east, north], degrees:
- Western Mediterranean: [-6, 35, 10, 45]
- New York / Lower Bay: [-74.32, 40.40, -73.67, 41.00]
- Singapore: [103.55, 1.05, 104.17, 1.51]
- Eilat / northern Gulf of Aqaba: [34.73, 29.32, 35.20, 29.79]
The three urban refinements replace only their local geometry; service port
coordinates and validated ocean/canal corridors remain unchanged.
Source resolution is 1:10 million cartographic scale, NOT ten-meter accuracy.
See docs/GEOGRAPHY.md and docs/build_geography.py for reproduction and limitations.
